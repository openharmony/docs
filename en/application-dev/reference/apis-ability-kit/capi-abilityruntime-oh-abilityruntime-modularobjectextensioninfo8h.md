## Overview

Represents the information handle of a single ModularObjectExtensionAbility. The handle points to the complete information of a ModularObjectExtensionAbility, including attributes such as the launch mode, process mode, thread mode, component name ([AbilityBase_Element](capi-abilitybase-element.md)), and disabled state.
